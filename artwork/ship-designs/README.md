# Approved ship artwork

These transparent 60×56 PNGs preserve the approved ferry, fishing trawler and
tanker concepts, with empty decks and the original twenty small commit
containers. They remain unchanged as historical approval references for the
editable integer-grid artwork in `src/git_ship/sprites.py`. The trawler
references show its hook at rest.

Current rendering groups four commits into one large container, so twenty
commits now appear as five large containers. Empty-reference comparisons remain
pixel-exact; loaded-reference comparisons mask the reserved cargo bank to verify
that the approved hull name panel and superstructure geometry remain unchanged.
Narrower ships still fit their superstructure beside that bank. Small containers
are 5×4 logical pixels, large containers 8×7, and lettering keeps its native size.
