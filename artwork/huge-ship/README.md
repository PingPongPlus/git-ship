# Approved huge ship artwork

These original transparent 300×110 PNGs preserve the approved huge cargo hull
at 0, 24, 72 and 120 commits, with six commits per large container. The editable
integer-grid source is `src/git_ship/huge_ship.py`.

The production sprite adds an exact total on the left of the hull and an overflow
counter on the right when more than thirty large containers are needed. Remainder
commits are drawn as individual small boxes in their own bay. The approved hull,
bridge, cargo geometry and centered name remain unchanged.

The animation uses the separately approved calmer wake: one gentle roll per
loop, broad broken displacement foam, and a low bow curl without flying spray.

`language-containers.png` records the six approved 27×10 original pixel designs
in order: Java, Python, JavaScript, Rust, C++, and Git. Their editable source is
`src/git_ship/cargo_art.py`. Other languages use a neutral panel with a short
language label; mixed leftovers use `MIX`.
